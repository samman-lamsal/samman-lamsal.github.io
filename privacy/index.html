Privacy & transparency

Privacy.

Information about this studio portfolio website and the services used when you contact us or watch a preview.

Game and app website

This portfolio website does not offer account creation and does not intentionally use analytics trackers or third-party advertising scripts. Games and apps reached through external platforms follow their own privacy rules.

Contact form

The contact page sends your name, email address and message through FormSubmit, a third-party email relay, to the developer's inbox. The information is used to read and reply to your message. It is not stored by this static website or used for a newsletter. FormSubmit processes the submission to deliver it, subject to its own policies.

Video and web fonts

Rival Guys gameplay previews are hosted by YouTube. The embedded player is only loaded after you choose to play the preview, at which point YouTube may receive information from your browser. You can open the video directly on YouTube instead. Some text styles are loaded from Google Fonts, which can receive browser requests when you visit.

External platforms

Game and app buttons may take you to independent third-party services, including CrazyGames, Facebook, Google Play, Playgama, itch.io and iDev.Games. Their own terms and privacy policies apply.

Game-specific policies

Individual games and apps may have platform-specific privacy policies or data disclosures. Refer to the official store or platform listing for details about each title.
