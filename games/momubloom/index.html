About MomuBloom

MomuBloom is an Android parenting/lifestyle utility with weekly milestone information, a personal diary, general nutrition references, customizable habit reminders and a quick-access support shortcut.

Key features

Weekly insights and milestone tracking
Private lifestyle diary stored locally on-device
General nutrition reference guides
Custom hydration/vitamin reminders and quick-access support shortcut
