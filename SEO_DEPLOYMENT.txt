3. In Google Search Console, inspect https://masarpstudio.com/ and click Request indexing.
4. Submit https://masarpstudio.com/sitemap.xml in Search Console > Sitemaps.
5. In Bing Webmaster Tools, request indexing for the homepage after deployment.
