About Omelette Chef Kitchen

Omelette Chef Kitchen is a casual cooking game on Google Play where players drag eggs to the pan, prepare customer orders and complete time-based missions.

Key features

Drag-and-drop cooking controls
Customer order matching
Time-management missions
Level progression with colorful kitchen visuals

Developer

Omelette Chef Kitchen is part of the MASARP Studio portfolio by independent game developer Samman Lamsal.
