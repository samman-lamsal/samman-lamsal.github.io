About the game.

Jelly Poppys is a match-3 candy puzzle game on Playgama with hundreds of levels, level-specific objectives, coins, power-ups and bonus mini-games.

Created under MASARP STUDIO, the independent development brand of Samman Lamsal.

The experience

Key features.

Hundreds of objective-based match-3 levels

Coins used to support progression
Bonus mini-games accessible from the main menu
