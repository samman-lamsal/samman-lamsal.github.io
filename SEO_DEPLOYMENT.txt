Included in this build:

After deploying:
1. Confirm https://masarpstudio.com/favicon.ico loads the MASARP logo (not the 404 page).
2. Confirm https://masarpstudio.com/sitemap.xml loads normally.
3. In Google Search Console, inspect https://masarpstudio.com/ and click Request indexing.
4. Submit https://masarpstudio.com/sitemap.xml in Search Console > Sitemaps.
5. In Bing Webmaster Tools, request indexing for the homepage after deployment.

Important: technical SEO can improve crawling/indexing, but no code change can guarantee a particular Google ranking. Brand ranking often improves after Google recrawls the site and collects stronger authority/link signals.
