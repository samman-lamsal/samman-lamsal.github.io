About Sammu Craft

Sammu Craft is a browser adventure with Minecraft-style visuals. Its public listing highlights obstacle courses, resource gathering, tool crafting, combat, parkour, creative sandbox building and multiple biomes.

Key features

Voxel-style browser worlds
Resource gathering and crafting
Parkour, sandbox building and combat modes
Gold rewards and an in-game store
