WEBSITE POLICY

Privacy

Privacy information for this static portfolio website.

Portfolio website

This website does not use account creation, analytics trackers or advertising scripts. It is a lightweight static portfolio website. The only exception is the contact form described below.

Contact form

The contact page lets you send a message directly to Samman Lamsal. Submissions are delivered by FormSubmit, a third-party email-relay service, straight to Samman's inbox. The name, email address, and message you provide are used only to read and reply to your message. They are not stored on this website, added to a mailing list, or shared with anyone else. Your browser also sets a small theme preference (light or dark) using local storage; this stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

External platforms

Game and app buttons link to third-party platforms including Facebook, Google Play, Playgama, itch.io and iDev.Games. Those services apply their own privacy policies.

Game-specific policies

Individual games and apps may have platform-specific privacy policies or data disclosures. The official store/platform listing should be treated as the current source for each title.
